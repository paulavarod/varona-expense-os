# Expense Categories

## Purpose

This file defines the professional expense categories for Varona GmbH.

These categories should be used consistently in TaxHacker or any future expense system.

## Core Categories

```txt
Banking Fees
Brokerage Fees

AI Tools

```

## Category Details

### Banking Fees

Wise fees, bank account fees, transfer fees.

### Brokerage Fees

IBKR fees, trading platform fees, broker account costs.

### Software Subscriptions

General business software.

Examples:

```txt
Google Workspace
Microsoft 365
Notion
Dropbox
DocuSign
```

### AI Tools

AI-related tools.

Examples:

```txt
OpenAI
Claude
Perplexity
Make AI modules
Other AI tools
```

### Email and Domain

Company domain, email hosting, DNS, website hosting.

Examples:

```txt
GoDaddy
Squarespace
Hostinger
Google Domains
```

### Legal and Compliance

Legal review, compliance advice, regulatory review, contract review.

### Accounting and Fiduciary

Accountant, fiduciary, bookkeeping, tax preparation.

### Company Formation

Incorporation costs, notary, commercial register.

### LEI and Registrations

LEI registration, renewals and identifiers.

### Professional Services

Consultants, advisors, external professional support.

### Office and Administration

Basic company administration expenses.

### Travel

Business travel costs. Must include business purpose.

### Meals and Business Meetings

Meals linked to business activity. Must include business purpose and attendees where possible.

### Education and Research

Courses, books, reports, professional learning.

### Investment Research

Market research, investment reports, data sources.

### Trading Data and Tools

TradingView, data providers, trading analytics tools.

### Real Estate Project Costs

Costs linked to real estate opportunity analysis.

### SPV Project Costs

Costs linked to SPV creation, structuring or evaluation.

### Marketing and Website

Website, brand assets, design, content, marketing tools.

### Hardware and Equipment

Laptop, monitor, office equipment.

### Taxes and Government Fees

Official government fees and taxes.

### Needs Review

Use when category is uncertain.

## Review Rule

When uncertain, use:

```txt
Needs Review
```

Do not force a category.
